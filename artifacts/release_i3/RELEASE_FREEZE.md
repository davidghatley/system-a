## Contract and decision

One whole-turn text state plus exactly one `next_action` choice, with criterion order `edit, execute, other_tool, read, respond_or_finish, search` and exact frozen instructions/descriptions from `release/i3/inference.py`. Fixed six-label reporting order `read, search, edit, execute, other_tool, respond_or_finish`. Laya maximum length 512, head length 192, frozen renderer/sequence rules. Outputs: raw softmax and a dev-fitted scalar-temperature view from the same forward pass. Temperature `6.203483215500731` from `release/i3/calibration.json`; no assertion of calibrated deployment probabilities. All other question schemas, extra gold/provenance fields, and changed criteria order/descriptions are rejected.

Raw seed 42: `0.445265`, `0.678571`, `3.405566`, `0.532692`. This met the prospectively stated release-use rule in `research/30_i3_release_protocol_amendment.md`; the final temperature was refit on all dev. Six-class macro-F1 includes two classes with no dev or historical-final support (`other_tool`, `respond_or_finish`). There is no evidence of arbitrary-schema performance, optimal action choice, or task completion.
